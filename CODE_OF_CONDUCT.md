# Code of Conduct

OBJY DB welcomes constructive participation from everyone.

Be respectful, direct, and considerate. Discuss technical decisions on their
merits, accept good-faith disagreement, and avoid harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, threats, or disclosure of another person's private
information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms the project or its community. Report conduct concerns privately
through the repository maintainers rather than in a public issue.
